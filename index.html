ABOUT ME

Video editing isn't just what I do.
It's what I see.

I'm a self-taught video editor and motion designer with 4+ years of experience turning ideas into visual stories.

Editing started as a passion for me, and it never became something I got bored of. I enjoy every side of the process — from short-form content and cinematic editing to motion graphics, animation, VFX and visual storytelling.

My biggest focus is vision.

I don't just edit from the creator's perspective. I think about the person watching. How will they feel? What will make them stop? What will keep them watching? What should the next frame feel like?

That's the perspective I bring into every edit.

MY APPROACH

See it from the viewer's side.

I try to experience every edit as the audience would — because a technically good video means nothing if nobody wants to watch it.
